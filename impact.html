Impact經營成果

Decisions that moved the business.真正推動事業前進的決策。

Four areas where strategy met execution — each told as the challenge faced, the action taken, and the business impact.四個策略與執行交會的領域——每一項都以面對的挑戰、採取的行動與帶來的經營影響來呈現。

01 — Global Expansion01 — 全球拓展

Opening markets across two regions.在兩大區域開拓市場。

Standing up a Silicon Valley presence在矽谷建立據點

Challenge挑戰

Compete for customers and talent in the world's most demanding technology market with no local footprint.在全球競爭最激烈的科技市場中，沒有在地基礎卻要爭取客戶與人才。

Action行動

Built and led a local team from the ground up, and established direct customer and partner relationships.從零建立並領導在地團隊，並建立直接的客戶與夥伴關係。

Business Impact經營影響

A credible regional presence that anchored expansion and shortened the path to key accounts.建立可信的區域據點，成為擴張的支點並縮短了切入關鍵客戶的路徑。

Connecting Asia and North America連結亞洲與北美

Challenge挑戰

Align very different buying cultures and operating rhythms across two regions.在兩大區域之間，協調截然不同的採購文化與營運節奏。

Action行動

Designed an expansion framework that sequenced markets, partners, and resourcing.設計擴張框架，為市場、夥伴與資源配置排定順序。

Business Impact經營影響

Supported sustained global business growth without overextending the organization.在不過度透支組織的前提下，支撐持續的全球業務成長。

02 — Business Transformation02 — 業務轉型

Bringing AI into how the business runs.把 AI 帶進事業的運作方式。

AI for executive productivityAI 賦能高階生產力

Challenge挑戰

Leadership time consumed by synthesis and reporting instead of judgment.領導者的時間被資訊整合與報告消耗，而非用於判斷。

Action行動

Promoted AI adoption for executive workflows — briefing, analysis, and decision prep.推動 AI 應用於高階工作流程——簡報、分析與決策準備。

Business Impact經營影響

Faster, better-informed decisions and a model others in the organization could follow.決策更快、更有依據，並為組織其他成員提供可遵循的範本。

Digital workflow and change數位工作流程與變革

Challenge挑戰

Manual, fragmented processes that did not scale with the business.人工且零散的流程，無法隨事業規模擴張。

Action行動

Led digital workflow redesign as organizational change, not just tooling.把數位工作流程重新設計視為組織變革，而非僅是導入工具。

Business Impact經營影響

More consistent execution and capacity freed for higher-value work.執行更一致，並釋放出投入更高價值工作的能量。

03 — Financial Strategy03 — 財務策略

Finance as fuel for growth.以財務作為成長的燃料。

Funding expansion為擴張籌資

Challenge挑戰

Growth ambitions outpaced available capital.成長企圖超出當下可運用的資本。

Action行動

Led two financing rounds, aligning capital structure with the growth plan.主導兩輪融資，使資本結構與成長計畫一致。

Business Impact經營影響

Secured the resources to expand while protecting the balance sheet.在守住資產負債表的同時，取得擴張所需資源。

Tax efficiency by design以架構實現稅務效率

Challenge挑戰

An effective tax rate of 37% was eroding reinvestable earnings.37% 的有效稅率侵蝕了可再投資的盈餘。

Action行動

Restructured the tax position through a deliberate, compliant strategy.透過審慎且合規的策略，重新調整稅務架構。

Business Impact經營影響

Effective tax rate improved from 37% to 18%, freeing capital for growth.有效稅率由 37% 降至 18%，釋放出可投入成長的資本。

04 — Governance & Public Company Experience04 — 治理與上市公司經驗

Building trust that scales with the business.建立能隨事業規模成長的信任。

Public-company discipline上市公司的紀律

Challenge挑戰

Meet the transparency and control standards expected of a public company.達到上市公司在透明度與內控上的標準。

Action行動

Operated within rigorous governance, reporting, and risk frameworks.在嚴謹的治理、申報與風險框架內運作。

Business Impact經營影響

Credibility with investors, partners, and customers that underwrote growth.在投資人、夥伴與客戶間建立可支撐成長的信譽。

Risk as a growth enabler把風險管理化為成長助力

Challenge挑戰

Pursue ambitious growth without taking on fragile risk.追求積極成長，卻不承擔脆弱的風險。

Action行動

Embedded risk management and capital strategy into business decisions.將風險管理與資本策略內嵌於經營決策之中。

Business Impact經營影響

Sustainable, defensible growth rather than fragile expansion.實現可持續、可防禦的成長，而非脆弱的擴張。
