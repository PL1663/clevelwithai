Leadership Journey領導歷程

A capability that compounded.一段不斷累積複利的能力演進。

Not a timeline of titles — an evolution of capability. Each stage added a discipline, and together they form the way I build and run a business today.這不是職稱的時間軸，而是能力的演進。每一個階段都累積一項核心能力，最終構成我今日經營事業的方式。

Global Business國際業務→ Strategy策略→ Finance財務→ AI TransformationAI 轉型→ Business Leadership事業經營
01

Global Business國際業務

What I learned我學到什麼

How real demand forms across cultures and markets, and that trust is the currency of international business.真實需求如何在不同文化與市場中形成，而信任是國際業務真正的貨幣。

What I built我建立了什麼

Cross-border customer relationships and partnerships across Asia and North America.橫跨亞洲與北美的跨境客戶關係與合作夥伴網絡。

How it shaped my leadership如何形塑我的領導

I lead from the market inward — decisions start with the customer, not the org chart.我從市場由外而內地領導——決策從客戶出發，而非從組織圖出發。

02

Strategy策略

What I learned我學到什麼

Direction is cheap; execution is the strategy. Focus is the hardest, most valuable decision.方向不值錢，執行才是真正的策略；聚焦是最難、也最有價值的決定。

What I built我建立了什麼

Market-entry and expansion frameworks that turned ambition into sequenced, fundable steps.將企圖心轉化為可排序、可融資步驟的市場進入與擴張框架。

How it shaped my leadership如何形塑我的領導

I translate vision into operating priorities a team can actually act on.我把願景轉譯成團隊真正能執行的營運優先順序。

03

Finance財務

What I learned我學到什麼

Capital, tax, and risk are not back-office functions — they decide what a business can attempt.資本、稅務與風險不是後勤功能，而是決定一門事業能嘗試什麼的關鍵。

What I built我建立了什麼

Two financing rounds and a tax structure that lifted the effective rate from 37% to 18%.兩輪融資，以及將有效稅率由 37% 降至 18% 的稅務架構。

How it shaped my leadership如何形塑我的領導

I see the P&L and balance sheet as instruments of growth, not just control.我把損益表與資產負債表視為成長工具，而不僅是控管工具。

04

AI TransformationAI 轉型

What I learned我學到什麼

AI's leverage is in judgment and workflow at the top, not novelty at the edges.AI 的槓桿在於決策層的判斷與工作流程，而非邊緣的新奇應用。

What I built我建立了什麼

An executive AI productivity system and digital workflows adopted across the organization.一套高階 AI 生產力系統，以及在組織內推行的數位工作流程。

How it shaped my leadership如何形塑我的領導

I treat transformation as a change-management problem, led from the front.我把轉型視為由前線親自帶領的變革管理課題。

05

Business Leadership事業經營

What I learned我學到什麼

A business scales on trusted people and clear ownership, not on the leader's bandwidth.事業靠的是值得信任的人與清晰的當責，而非領導者個人的時間。

What I built我建立了什麼

A Silicon Valley team built from scratch, with delegation and ownership designed in.從零打造的矽谷團隊，並從設計之初就植入授權與當責。

How it shaped my leadership如何形塑我的領導

I connect strategy, customers, capital, and people into one operating system for growth.我把策略、客戶、資本與人才整合為一套驅動成長的營運系統。
